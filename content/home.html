Five steps from idea to source

  1. Write the idea down. One sentence: who it is for and what it should do.
  2. Ask for a bounded first draft. Name the files and the acceptance criteria. How
  3. Review the diff. Apply what fits, reject what does not. How
  4. Capture a checkpoint. Keep a way back before the next experiment.
  5. Export your files. ZIP or JSON, ready for the tools you choose.
