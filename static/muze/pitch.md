# MuZe V6 pitch outline

1. **Problem** · Agent outputs are hard to approve and reconstruct when actions span tools.
2. **Solution** · Run agents in a self-hosted workspace with reviewable proposals and receipts.
3. **Market** · Initial hypothesis: owner-operated teams that need customer follow-up with an audit trail.
4. **Product** · Show a fixture lead, template draft, human decision and evidence verification.
5. **Traction** · Add confirmed pilot and usage metrics before submission.
6. **Business** · Test willingness to pay for managed hosting, support and integration work.
7. **Moat** · Self-hosting, payload-bound approval and independently checkable evidence.
8. **Ask** · Recruit pilot operators and explore Meta integrations, subject to provider approval.
