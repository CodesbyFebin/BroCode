# Support

- **Questions about the guides or the site:** open a [Discussion](https://github.com/CodesbyFebin/BroCode/discussions).
- **A mistake in a guide or a broken page:** open an issue with the *Content correction* or *Bug report* template.
- **Security issues:** do not open a public issue; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Feature ideas for the BroCODE app:** see the [roadmap](https://codingagent.in/roadmap/) (planned, not shipped) and add to a Discussion.

There is no paid support and no guaranteed response time.
